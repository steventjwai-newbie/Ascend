# Alpha validation roadmap

## Now — evidence artifact

- Demonstrate a real, anonymized supplier-receipt yield case.
- Keep every conclusion linked to a source and calculation.
- Catch unit, arithmetic, row-assignment and plausibility errors locally.
- Publish a no-login dashboard for MAIC judging.

## Next — Cafe Q1 pilot

- Capture receipt weights, portion outputs and unresolved by-products consistently.
- Connect sales and recipe demand to targeted stock checks.
- Measure alert usefulness, false alerts and owner review time over 14 days.
- Treat only physically or documentarily verified values as loss or savings.

## Later — repeatability

- Validate a second outlet and a different POS/export format.
- Expand supplier-product, ingredient-form, WIP and recipe linkages.
- Add menu engineering and low-friction staff verification tasks.
- Develop ESG reporting only from measured waste and auditable evidence.

## Product gate

Continue expanding only if Alpha repeatedly finds material exceptions early enough to change an operating decision while keeping normal owner review below approximately five minutes per day.
